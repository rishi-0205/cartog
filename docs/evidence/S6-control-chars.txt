What the fix does:

* New `cartog/core/text.py::sanitize_console_text()` removes complete ANSI
  sequences (CSI, OSC terminated by BEL or ST, and two-byte escapes), then all
  C0 controls except tab and newline, plus DEL and C1. This is why `[31m`
  cannot survive as visible text after the ESC byte is gone.
* `cartog/cli/shared.py` exports `SanitizingConsole`; the shared `console` is
  an instance, so every existing `console.print` site is covered. `str` and
  `Text` arguments are sanitized, and a `rich.table.Table` is deep-copied with
  its columns, header, footer, title and caption sanitized.
* `cartog/render/views.py::generate_digest()` returns
  `sanitize_console_text("\n".join(lines))`, which also covers names and paths
  inside the digest tables.
* `docs/dev/PACKAGE_LAYOUT.md` lists the new `cartog/core/text.py` module.
