1. PROBLEM & DIAGNOSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In cartog/cli.py:_resolve_graph(), when searching for existing graph.json,
   the candidate path `Path(out) / p.name / "graph.json"` was omitted. As a
   result, invoking `cartog flow samples/flask ...` with default out="cartog-out"
   failed to find `cartog-out/flask/graph.json` and fell through to directory
   re-parsing.
2. When falling through to in-memory building `if p.is_dir():`, `_resolve_graph`
   called `_execute_parse()` followed by `build_graph()` with `scip_result=None`.
   Without SCIP resolution, 0 `calls` edges were populated in the in-memory graph,
   causing flow traces to terminate prematurely at depth 1.
