## Systematic misses (honest accounting)

1. **Object-literal service chains** (`teamService.getTeamById`,
   `getTournamentService.getAllTournaments`, `invitesService.applyToTournament`,
   etc.) — SCIP resolves some but not all. Roughly 12 of 74 served routes
   have 0 calls because of this. Documented limitation.
2. **`app.use` mounts (10)** + inline `/health` (1) — no serves edges (Rank 9).
3. **External-only calls** — `redis.incr()`, `prisma.*`, `appleSignin.verifyIdToken`,
   `OAuth2Client.verifyIdToken` — correctly not emitted as in-repo calls.

## No fabricated edges

Every produces/consumes edge in the graph maps to a real `*.add("name", ...)`
or `new Worker("name", fn)` in source (3 channels, 12 produces, 3 consumes).
No false channels were found.

## Conclusion

All 85 routes were manually reviewed. 74 have correct handler edges; about
62 of those have complete in-repo call chains; ~12 are shallow due to
object-literal resolution gaps; 11 have no handler by design (mounts/inline).
Cartog's graph is accurate — no wrong edges, but uneven recall on
re-exported service objects.
