## Labelling

Every evidence line was opened in the checkout. Verdict rule: TP when an
evidence line is a real call (or instantiation) from caller to callee, FP
otherwise.

| repo | call edges | sampled | TP | FP | precision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| webscrapper (e1901a1, local checkout) | 219 | 30 | 24 | 6 | 0.800 |
| ky (3541888, samples.lock) | 238 | 30 | 26 | 4 | 0.867 |
| both | 457 | 60 | 50 | 10 | 0.833 |

All ten FPs are the Rank 1 rule: an attribute reference inside a call
expression is classified as a call, and the callee is the symbol enclosing the
attribute's definition.

- four `test_* -> setUp` edges, because `self.parser` is assigned in `setUp`
  (tests/test_parser.py:106/189/202/214);
- `Fetcher.close -> Fetcher.__init__`, because `self.session` is assigned in
  `__init__` (scraper/core/fetcher.py:106);
- `Scraper._looks_js_rendered -> ScrapeResponse`, because `response.raw_html`
  is a dataclass field (scraper/core/scraper.py:194);
- three `Ky.#method -> Ky` edges, because `this.#retryLimit`,
  `this.#hookResponses` and `this.#abortController` are class fields
  (Ky.ts:1225/1057/937);
- `Ky.create -> Ky` at Ky.ts:367 is a private-field read. The real
  `new Ky(input, initHookOptions)` is at Ky.ts:357 and is a `references` edge to
  `Ky.constructor`, not a `calls` edge - the `calls` edge exists only because
  of the field reads.

The full 60-row JSON with one reason per FP is
`docs/dev/verification/call-sample.json`; the test recomputes every count and
precision from the verdicts.
